DecisionGraph

A Company Brain on FalkorDB. It reads the company's docs, tickets and chat into one graph, answers with quoted sources, and refuses to guess. All data is synthetic (Larkspur Systems).
Nemotron routes your question to one read-only graph tool. Parameterized Cypher reads FalkorDB, a fixed policy decides, and every answer cites the records it came from.

Keep it current: add a new record

A new record goes through the same pipeline: Nemotron extracts facts, code checks each quote against the text, and the graph is updated. Then ask again.
